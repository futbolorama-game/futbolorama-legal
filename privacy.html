Privacy Policy for Copa Futbolorama

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 App ID: com.futbolorama.arcade

This Privacy Policy explains how Futbolorama Studio (Independent Developer) (“we”, “us”) handles information in relation to the mobile game Copa Futbolorama (package name com.futbolorama.arcade). The game is designed for football fans of all ages, including children and families.

1. Information and Use

The game stores settings, game progress, cosmetics, and purchase-entitlement records locally on your device to provide gameplay and remember your choices. Gameplay diagnostic measurements may be kept locally or in memory. Android Auto Backup is disabled in the app manifest (allowBackup="false").

Advertising and in-app purchases depend on the version and services enabled in the app. The disclosures below apply when those services are enabled.

If advertising is enabled in the released app, we configure Google AdMob universally for children and families: requests apply child-directed treatment (TFCD), under-age-of-consent handling (TFUA), a G maximum ad content rating, blocked sensitive ad categories, and non-personalized ad requests only (npa=1). The app also removes Android Advertising ID permissions (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and android.permission.AD_ID) from its manifest so the Android Advertising ID is not requested or collected. Even without personalized advertising, Google Mobile Ads and its certified SDK components may process limited technical information—such as IP address for ad delivery and coarse location, basic network state, ad impressions/interactions, and app diagnostics—to deliver contextual ads, enforce frequency limits, ensure security, and prevent fraud. Where required by regional law, the app surfaces a consent or Privacy Options control inside the Audio / Settings menu. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

If purchases are enabled, Google Play processes payments and may process purchase history, account, and transaction information under its own policies. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. The game may receive a product identifier, purchase token, signature, and purchase state to verify and restore entitlements, storing an entitlement record locally. Google Play controls its own transaction records. See Google Play privacy information.

If you contact us directly via email, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address and request details, and use it solely to respond, verify the request when necessary, and meet legal obligations. Please do not send payment card information via email.

2. Sharing, Security and Retention

We disclose limited technical information to Google and its service providers only as required to deliver contextual non-personalized advertising and process Google Play purchases when enabled, and may disclose information when legally required or to protect rights and prevent fraud. We do not sell personal information or build behavioral advertising profiles.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect user communications; Google states its Mobile Ads SDK uses TLS in transit. Local game data remains on your device until cleared, overwritten, or removed when the app is uninstalled.

We retain privacy correspondence for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Your Choices and Rights

You may clear app storage in your Android device settings or uninstall the game at any time to remove local data. This may reset local game progress; purchases may be restored via Google Play. Where available in your region, you can also review choices via the in-game Privacy Options control inside Audio Settings.

Parents, guardians, and users may contact us at futbolorama@protonmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of any personal information we control. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. Google controls its own account data and provides user privacy controls.

EEA and UK Users: Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

California Residents: Under the CCPA/CPRA, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Submit any requests to futbolorama@protonmail.com.

4. Children and Families Privacy

Copa Futbolorama is intended for football fans of all ages, including children and families. The game has no user accounts, no open chat, and no user-generated public content. To protect younger players universally without relying on personalized tracking, all users receive child-directed, non-personalized, G-rated contextual advertising settings with Android Advertising ID permissions removed.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what you choose to send if you contact support by email, or what platform services strictly need to deliver non-personalized contextual ads or parent-authorized Google Play purchases. Parents and guardians can contact us at futbolorama@protonmail.com with questions about a child’s information or to request access to or deletion of any personal information we control.

5. Changes and Contact Information

We may update this policy as our practices or legal obligations change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page and provide any notice required by law.
