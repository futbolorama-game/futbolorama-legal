Privacy Policy for Copa Futbolorama

Effective Date: November 11, 2026 App ID: com.futbolorama.arcade

This Privacy Policy explains how Futbolorama Studio (Independent Developer) (“we”, “us”) handles information in relation to the mobile game Copa Futbolorama (package name com.futbolorama.arcade).

1. Information and Use

The game stores settings, game progress, cosmetics, and purchase-entitlement records locally on your device to provide gameplay and remember your choices. Gameplay diagnostic measurements may be kept locally or in memory.

Advertising and in-app purchases depend on the version and services enabled in the app. The disclosures below apply when those services are enabled.

If advertising is enabled in the released app, Google AdMob and its partners may receive device or advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and app diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and secure advertising and, where applicable, personalize ads. Non-personalized ads can still use information for measurement, frequency limits, and fraud prevention. Where available, the app may present a consent or privacy choices interface; Android advertising-ID controls and Google's ad settings also provide choices. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

If purchases are enabled, Google Play processes payments and may process purchase history, account, and transaction information under its own policies. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. The game may receive a product identifier, purchase token, signature, and purchase state to verify and restore entitlements, storing an entitlement record locally. Google Play controls its own transaction records. See Google Play privacy information.

If you contact us directly via email, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address and request details, and use it solely to respond, verify the request when necessary, and meet legal obligations. Please do not send payment card information via email.

2. Sharing, Security and Retention

We disclose information to Google and its service providers when the services described above are enabled, and may disclose information when legally required or to protect rights and prevent fraud.

We use reasonable safeguards to protect user communications; Google states its Mobile Ads SDK uses TLS in transit. Local game data remains on your device until cleared, overwritten, or removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to your device's backup settings.

We retain privacy correspondence for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Your Choices and Rights

You may clear app storage in your Android device settings or uninstall the game at any time to remove local data, subject to cloud backups. This may reset local game progress.

You may contact us at futbolorama@protonmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of any personal information we control. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request. Google controls its own account data and provides user privacy controls.

EEA and UK Users: Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, data portability, and to withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

California Residents: Under the CCPA/CPRA, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Submit any requests to futbolorama@protonmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at futbolorama@protonmail.com so we can investigate and delete any such information promptly.

5. Changes and Contact Information

We may update this policy as our practices or legal obligations change. We will update the effective date at the top of this page and provide any notice required by law.
